About Anu's Beauty Parlour

Vellore's trusted name in bridal artistry

Founded on the belief that every face tells its own story, Anu's Beauty Parlour has grown into Vellore's go-to studio for brides who want to look like the most elevated version of themselves — never like someone else.

Every appointment begins with a conversation, not a template: your skin tone and texture, the fabric and colour of your outfit, the lighting of your venue, and the mood you want to carry through the day all shape the look we create together.

Our artists are trained in HD and airbrush techniques and work only with premium, skin-friendly products, so your makeup photographs beautifully and stays flawless from the morning rituals to the final dance. Hygiene, punctuality, and honest advice are non-negotiable — because your day deserves nothing less.

From intimate functions to full wedding celebrations, we have styled hundreds of clients across Vellore, and every look you see on this page belongs to a real client, on her real day.
